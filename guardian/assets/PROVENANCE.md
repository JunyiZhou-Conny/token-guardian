# Prototype champion avatars

`champions.png` is a generated reference-based interpretation of the user's two supplied
Red Note screenshots of cute League of Legends champion avatars credited to
**AiBaker** (Red Note ID: AiBaker). The atlas contains Ahri, Jinx and Jhin, in that
order. It was generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-10. The committed
atlas is the asset used by the prototype; the two supplied screenshots are visual
references, rather than an artwork license.

## Cozy outfit variations

`champion-variants.png` adds coordinated outfit variations of the same Ahri,
Jinx, and Jhin avatars, in that order: Ahri's pastel star cloak, Jinx's blue and
pink hoodie with goggles, and Jhin's violet tailored coat. The transparent
three-column atlas was generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-11,
using `champions.png` as its visual reference. The original atlas is preserved.
